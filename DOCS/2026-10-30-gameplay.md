## Resulting behavior

- Death with a living teammate: recall all living owned Pikmin, including busy ones, to their own Onion; then dormancy. The sim and presentation keep running even with no living Olimar. After 20 seconds, store terrain-blocked survivors rather than abandoning them. A stage transition preserves living returners too.
- Stage transfers preserve colour and leaf/bud/flower stage for squad and Onion stock across all five colours. Existing Purple/White counters are fully cleared before each save.
- Quail landing: resolve descending sub-step floor contact before wall probing, so the floor cannot consume the horizontal boost as a false wall hit.
- Native RoR2 enemies can target ground groups of at least three Pikmin through the shared minion implementation: up to four targets per captain. Proxy hits enter the existing enemy area-damage path and kill real Pikmin. No solid proxy capsule obstructs Olimar. Pikmin enemies retain their own AI.
- Timed RoR2 shake attempts supplement combat-skill shakes: every 1 second, or 0.25 seconds for bosses. The configured latch protection still applies (default 2 seconds). Damage coefficients are unchanged for this pass.
- Full carrier teams suppress native lock markers for pellets, parts and treasures; releasing carriers permits locking again.
- Flying Onion menus accept Fill Onion; returns jump to the leg midpoint and climb its upper half.
- Raise/lower: hold X/Utility 0.6 seconds; a single tap never relocates. Double tap still dumps a flying Onion. Interact prompts retain the button.
- The initial Pikmin camera yaw rotates 90 degrees.
- Numbered drops: boss 20; elite or base health >=600: 10; other base health >=200 or radius >=1 metre: 5; otherwise 1. Use base health so level scaling cannot inflate pellet size. 20 is the largest numbered asset. Optional corpse-body carry weight retains its separate size setting.
